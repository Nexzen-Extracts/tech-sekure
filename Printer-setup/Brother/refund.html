Refund Policy

Last Updated: 07-02-2026

Thank you for choosing TechSekure. We want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase. This Refund Policy explains eligibility, the return process, timelines, and conditions for refunds.

1. General Refund Policy

Refunds are offered on select products and services under the conditions below:

Eligibility for Refund

  • You must contact us within 30 days of receiving your product.
  • Items must be in original condition and packaging.

Non-Refundable Items

  • Special/custom orders that cannot be restocked.
  • Used or damaged items (unless defective).
  • Digital/downloadable products.
  • Gift cards.

2. Return Process

  1. Contact Us: Email: info@techsekure.shop Phone: +1 (877) 496-7174
  2. Return Authorization (RA): RA number may be required. Returns without RA may be rejected.
  3. Shipping Instructions: You are responsible for return shipping unless the product is defective or our error.
  4. Restocking Fee: A **20% restocking fee** may apply depending on condition.
  5. Refund Timeline: Refunds are processed within 7–10 business days after inspection.

3. Damaged or Defective Products

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery.
  • Provide photos + order number.
  • Return shipping is covered if item is defective.
  • Replacement or refund will be issued after inspection.

4. Services

If you are unsatisfied with a service, contact us within 30 days. Refund eligibility depends on service completion.

Note: Services already completed may not be refundable.

5. Refund Method

  • Card Payments: 5–10 business days.
  • PayPal / others: refunded to original account.

6. Exchanges

Exchanges may be possible based on stock. If unavailable, a refund may be issued so you can place a new order.

7. Exceptions

  • Opened or used items (unless defective).
  • Digital/downloadable items.
  • Gift cards.
  • Final sale items.
