Refund Policy

Last Updated: 07-02-2026

Thank you for choosing TechSekure. We value transparency and customer satisfaction. This Refund Policy explains in detail the eligibility criteria, refund process, timelines, and conditions under which refunds or exchanges may be issued. For more information, please visit https://techsekure.shop or contact us at +1 (877) 496-7174 .

1. Policy Overview

  • This policy applies to all products and services purchased from TechSekure.
  • Refunds are subject to review and approval.
  • By placing an order, you agree to this Refund Policy.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

  • Requests must be submitted within 30 days of delivery or service completion.
  • Products must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging.
  • Valid proof of purchase is required.
  • Eligibility may vary based on product or service type.

3. Non-Refundable Items

  • Digital or downloadable products.
  • Gift cards or prepaid items.
  • Customized or special-order products.
  • Final sale or clearance items.
  • Items damaged due to misuse or negligence.

4. Return Authorization (RA) Process

  1. Contact us at info@techsekure.shop or call +1 (877) 496-7174.
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return.
  3. An RA number may be issued before returning the item.
  4. Returns without authorization may not be accepted.

5. Return Shipping & Responsibilities

  • Customers are responsible for return shipping unless the item is defective.
  • Items must be securely packaged.
  • We recommend using a trackable shipping service.

6. Inspection, Fees & Approval

  • All returned items are inspected upon receipt.
  • A restocking fee of up to 20% may apply.
  • Refund approval depends on inspection results.

7. Refund Processing Time

  • Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days.
  • Processing times may vary by payment provider.

8. Damaged or Defective Items

  • Report issues within 7 days of delivery.
  • Provide clear photos and order details.
  • If confirmed defective, replacement or refund will be issued.

9. Services & Digital Assistance

  • Service refund requests must be submitted within 30 days.
  • Refund eligibility depends on service progress.
  • Completed services may not be refundable.

10. Refund Method

  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
  • No cash or alternate refund options are available.

11. Abuse Prevention

  • Refund abuse or fraudulent activity may result in denial.
  • Repeated refund requests may trigger account review.

12. Policy Updates

This Refund Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in business practices, consumer protection laws, or advertising platform requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
