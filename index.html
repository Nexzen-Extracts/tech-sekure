Printer Setup

Follow these simple setup steps to get your printer installed and ready for smooth printing.

STEP 01

Unbox & Power On

Remove all packaging, connect the power cable, and turn on the printer.

STEP 02

Connect to Network

Connect your printer via Wi-Fi or USB depending on your setup preference.

STEP 03

Install Printer Driver

Download and install the latest driver from the official printer manufacturer website.

STEP 04

Test Print

Run a test print to confirm the printer is correctly installed and ready to use.

Printer Diagnosed

Follow these step-by-step diagnosed methods to quickly identify and resolve common printer issues.

STEP 01

Check Connections

Ensure your printer is properly connected via USB or Wi-Fi and powered on.

STEP 02

Verify Printer Status

Confirm the printer is online, set as default, and free from error alerts.

STEP 03

Update Printer Driver

Install or update the correct printer driver for better compatibility.

STEP 04

Test & Get Help

Run a test print. If issues persist, connect with a printer expert.
